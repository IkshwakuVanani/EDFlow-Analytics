# Demo Script

Use this script for a 60-90 second walkthrough.

## Opening

EDFlow Orchestrator is not another ED dashboard.
It is a capacity orchestration prototype that turns predicted boarding pressure into accountable bed-conversion work.

The first wedge is admitted ED boarders who need med-surg beds.

## Click Path

1. Open the Command Center.
2. Point to the KPI strip:
   - ED boarders
   - predicted boarding hours
   - beds unlockable
   - at-risk SLA tasks
3. Point to the capacity timeline.
   - The red band is the afternoon risk window.
   - The chart compares boarding pressure, available beds, and no-action prediction.
4. Point to the capacity flow.
   - This shows the chain from ED boarders to ready beds.
   - The bottleneck is not only bed assignment; it is discharge, cleaning, transport, staffing, and placement.
5. Click a blocker.
   - Blockers are ranked by estimated bed-hour impact, not by queue age.
6. In Action Details, click Mark Done.
   - The action state changes immediately, showing a closed-loop workflow rather than a passive report.
7. Click Simulate.
   - Adjust EVS, transport, staffing, or accelerated discharge levers.
   - Run the simulation to estimate boarding-hour reduction.
8. Click Evidence.
   - CMS public-data analytics remain available as supporting evidence.

## Closing

The goal is not to make hospitals more aware of crowding.
The goal is to make them faster at creating real usable capacity.

## Guardrail

This is a healthcare operations prototype. It does not make clinical decisions, diagnosis predictions, treatment recommendations, or patient-level risk predictions.
